# Business Recommendations

1. **Start a win-back campaign for the "At-Risk High Spenders" group.**
   These 704 customers used to spend a lot and are now going quiet — they
   should be the first priority, not a random discount to everyone.

2. **Create a loyalty program for top customers (Champions/High-Value
   Loyalists).** Since a small group brings in most of the revenue, it
   makes sense to reward them so they don't leave — free delivery, early
   access to sales, etc.

3. **Send a simple "check-in" message to new customers around day 30 and
   day 60 after their first order.** Since most customers drop off in the
   first 2-3 months, this is the window where a small nudge could help.

4. **Look into why Fashion has more returns than other categories.** It
   could be sizing charts, product photos, or descriptions not matching
   what customers expect.

5. **Don't rely only on Electronics and Mobiles for revenue.** Since these
   two categories make up 81% of revenue, it would help to run more
   promotions on other categories like Home & Kitchen or Beauty to
   balance this out.

6. **Make UPI the easiest and fastest checkout option**, since it's
   already the most-used payment method. Small improvements here (saved
   UPI ID, one-tap checkout) could improve the buying experience.

7. **Target marketing and offers mainly at the 25-34 age group**, since
   they already spend the most. A separate, simpler campaign can be used
   for older age groups instead of one generic message for everyone.

8. **Set up a basic churn-risk system**: customers with no purchase in
   90-180 days get a reminder email, and customers past 180 days get a
   stronger discount offer before they're considered fully "lost."

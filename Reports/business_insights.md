# Business Insights

1. **Revenue is not spread evenly across customers.** Just the top 25% of
   customers bring in around 61% of the total revenue. Most of the
   business's money comes from a small group of loyal buyers.

2. **The "At-Risk High Spenders" group is the biggest concern.** These are
   704 customers who used to spend a lot (₹9.67 Cr total) but haven't
   bought anything recently. This is the group most likely to leave, and
   they're worth the most if we can bring them back.

3. **Champions (top RFM segment) are only 17% of customers but bring in
   over 40% of the revenue.** This matches what the K-Means clustering
   found too, so two different methods agree on the same thing.

4. **Retention is okay, but not great — 63.6%.** That means more than 1
   in 3 customers haven't ordered anything in the last 6 months. This is
   a real number to pay attention to, not just a small detail.

5. **A lot of customers come back more than once (84.4% repeat rate), but
   many still stop ordering eventually.** So people aren't leaving after
   their first order — they're leaving after buying a few times.

6. **Retention drops fastest in the first 2-3 months after a customer's
   first order.** After that, it becomes harder to get them to come
   back. This means the early days after a first purchase matter a lot.

7. **UPI is the most used payment method**, making up about 37% of total
   revenue — way more than Credit Card, Debit Card, or COD.

8. **Fashion has the highest return/cancellation rate (16.7%)**, higher
   than Electronics or Mobiles. Something about sizing or product
   expectations might be causing this.

9. **Just 2 categories — Electronics and Mobiles & Accessories — make up
   81% of all revenue.** The business depends heavily on these two
   categories doing well.

10. **The 25-34 age group spends the most overall**, bringing in more
    revenue than any other age group by a good margin. This looks like
    the core customer group for the business.
